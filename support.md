# Sleeve Support

Need help with Sleeve? We're here to help.

## Account and Sign In

Sleeve currently uses **Sign in with Apple**.

If you're having trouble signing in, syncing your collection, or accessing your account, contact Sleeve Support using the email below.

## Collection and Sync

Your Sleeve collection can synchronize across your devices when you're signed in.

If something doesn't look right, please avoid deleting or recreating your collection before contacting us. Include a brief description of what happened and, if helpful, screenshots of what you're seeing.

Never send your Apple password, authentication codes, or other passwords to Sleeve Support.

## Card or Set Information

If you find a card, set, printing, image, or other catalog information that appears to be missing or incorrect, we'd like to know.

When reporting catalog issues, please include as much of the following as possible:

- Card name
- Set name
- Card number
- Language or printing, when relevant
- A screenshot of what you're seeing in Sleeve

## Market Information

Market prices and other collectible information can vary between providers and marketplaces.

If you believe Sleeve is showing information for the wrong card, printing, language, or market identity, please contact us with the card details and a screenshot.

## Privacy

Questions or requests concerning privacy can also be sent to Sleeve Support.

You can delete your Sleeve account from within the app.

For more information, see the Sleeve Privacy Policy.

We'll make reasonable efforts to review support requests as promptly as possible. Response times may vary.
