
# Sleeve Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 2, 2026**

Sleeve is a collection management app designed to help trading card collectors organize, preserve, explore, and share their collections.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sleeve collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information

Sleeve uses Sign in with Apple to create and authenticate your account.

When you use Sign in with Apple, Sleeve receives an identifier associated with your Apple account and may receive information that Apple makes available through the sign-in process, such as your name or an Apple private relay email address.

Sleeve does not require or maintain a separate Sleeve password.

### Collection Information

When you use Sleeve, we may store information you add to your collection, including:

- Cards you collect
- Albums, pages, and card arrangements
- Wishlist information
- Collection notes and other information you choose to provide
- Photos you choose to add to your collection
- Information about your collection history

This information may be synchronized through Sleeve's cloud services so that your collection can be restored and accessed across your devices.

### Profile and Social Information

If you choose to create a public Sleeve identity or use social features, we may store information such as:

- Your display name
- Your public handle
- Your profile photo
- Your profile biography
- Collectors you follow
- Your relationships with other collectors
- Collection pages or exhibits that you choose to publish

Your private collection is not made public merely because you have a Sleeve account. Content is made available to other collectors only through Sleeve features designed for public sharing or publication.

### Technical Information

Sleeve and its service providers may process technical information necessary to operate, secure, synchronize, and troubleshoot the service. This may include device and app information, authentication information, network requests, server logs, and error information.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide and operate Sleeve
- Authenticate your account
- Save and synchronize your collection
- Restore your collection on another device
- Provide collection, profile, publishing, and social features
- Retrieve card, catalog, and market information
- Maintain the security and reliability of the service
- Diagnose errors and improve Sleeve

## Card, Catalog, and Market Data

Sleeve may communicate with third-party data providers to retrieve information about trading cards, sets, artwork, catalog records, and market information.

These services may receive technical information associated with requests made by Sleeve. Sleeve does not intentionally send your private collection records to third-party card or market data providers unless necessary to provide a feature you request.

## Service Providers

Sleeve uses third-party infrastructure and services to operate certain features, including cloud hosting, authentication, storage, card data, and market data.

These providers may process information on Sleeve's behalf as necessary to provide their services.

## Public Content

Information that you intentionally publish through Sleeve's public or social features may be visible to other Sleeve users.

You should only publish information that you are comfortable making available to others.

## Tracking and Advertising

Sleeve does not use third-party advertising trackers to track you across apps or websites for targeted advertising.

Sleeve does not sell your personal information.

## Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Sleeve, maintain your account, comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service.

Information may also remain temporarily in backups or technical systems as part of normal service operations.

## Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Sleeve account from within the app.

Deleting your account is designed to remove the account and associated cloud data controlled by Sleeve, subject to information that must be retained for legitimate legal, security, fraud-prevention, or operational reasons.

Data stored locally on your devices may remain until the app or local data is removed.

## Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information handled by Sleeve.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children's Privacy

Sleeve is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to Sleeve inappropriately, please contact us so that we can review and address the situation.

## Your Choices

Depending on the features you use, you can:

- Choose what cards and information to add to Sleeve
- Choose whether to create or maintain a public profile
- Choose what collection content to publish
- Manage your collection and profile information
- Sign out of Sleeve
- Delete your Sleeve account

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Sleeve evolves.

If we make material changes, we may provide notice through the app or by updating this page. The effective date at the top of this policy indicates when it was last updated.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Sleeve's privacy practices, please use the contact information provided on the Sleeve Support page.
